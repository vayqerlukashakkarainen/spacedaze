# SpaceDaze Ultimates

Ultimates charge through combat and should create a short, unmistakable room-changing moment. Every activation needs a readable tell, a buildup, a powerful payoff, and clean visual recovery. Camera shake and screen flashes respect the player's display settings.

## Implemented roster

### Phase Nova

Release a fully charged phase wave that devastates nearby hostiles.

- Role: immediate radial damage
- Feedback: purple shockwave, explosion burst, screen shake
- Scaling: radius, damage, charge recovery

### Graviton Collapse

Launch a singularity at the cursor. It pulls enemies, hostile projectiles, rocks, barrels, and loose wreckage into a bright event horizon before detonating and throwing survivors outward.

- Role: crowd control, object interaction, delayed area damage
- Buildup: contracting rings, rotating gravity spokes, escalating shake
- Payoff: white core flash, purple shockwave, large explosion and outward knockback
- Scaling: pull radius, collapse damage, charge recovery

### Ghost Fleet

Record the player's movement and firing for four seconds, then summon three phase echoes that replay the maneuver from offset positions.

- Role: weapon amplification and movement mastery
- Buildup: cyan recording pulse and trailing phase samples
- Payoff: three ship echoes materialize with a screen flash and replay every recorded shot
- Scaling: echo projectile damage, replay speed, charge recovery

### Scrap Colossus

Pull nearby persistent wreckage together into a temporary allied warship. The colossus follows the player and fires seeking scrap bolts before breaking back into persistent parts.

- Role: summon, sustained damage, wreckage economy
- Buildup: nearby parts streak inward while the hull assembles
- Payoff: heavy arrival shake, autonomous volleys, explosive disassembly
- Scaling: duration, weapon damage, charge recovery
- Scrap contribution: more consumed parts add additional cannons

## Planned roster

### Event Horizon

Create a large slow-time zone at the cursor. Enemies and hostile projectiles nearly freeze while the player remains at full speed. The field ends with a damaging phase shock.

### Void Corridor

Draw a line through the cursor and phase-jump repeatedly along it, damaging everything crossed before releasing the player with extreme velocity.

### Emergency Refit

Snap nearby wreckage onto the ship as temporary armor. It restores hull and creates destructible plates that retaliate when broken.

### Orbital Execution

Mark the enemy nearest the cursor. Targeting beams converge before an enormous rail strike penetrates the target and continues through the room.

### Phase Tempest

Become untouchable and phase-jump between nearby enemies, striking each target before returning to the activation point.

### Dead Signal

Disable enemy weapons and shields, destroy hostile projectiles, and make affected enemies vulnerable for several seconds.

### Debris Storm

Pull loose rocks, cover, barrels, and ship parts into a protective orbit, then launch everything toward the cursor.
